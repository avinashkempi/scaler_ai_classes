The Analyst Files · Case #02
THE 40-CHART DASHBOARD
Food Delivery

Setup

A week after the last incident, Aditi — data analyst at a food-delivery app — sits in the Monday review. The VP stops the meeting: “What is the one number we should all be rowing toward — and underneath it, what should each team actually watch?”

Evidence

  • The company dashboard has quietly grown to 40-plus charts.
  • Marketing is thrilled about 2 million app downloads; content tracks video uploads; support tracks ticket volume.
  • Meanwhile, repeat orders are quietly slipping — and plenty of those 2 million users opened the app once and vanished.

The Mystery

Which number should Aditi propose as the company's guiding metric?

Make Your Move

Choose your first move before continuing. There’s no need to know the answer yet — tap an option to see why it’s worth considering.

Why this move

The fix isn't more data — it's a hierarchy: one North Star at the top, a few Level-1 (primary) metrics that feed it, and granular Level-2 (supporting) metrics underneath.

Your mission: come to class able to explain why “2 million downloads” tells nobody what to do next.

🎯 Your mission: come to class with a first move in mind.

Section 2

Real-World Use Cases

Airbnb — Booked NightsA North Star that only rises when both guest and host win. Sign-ups would look good while the marketplace stalled.
Fixing a bloated dashboardCollapsing 40 equal charts into North Star → L1 → L2 gives every team one number to own and a clear line to the company goal.
Influencer marketing reality check5M followers can still yield a 0.001% CTR. Brands that pay on conversions instead of reach are switching from vanity to actionable.
FAANG product interviews“What metrics would you use for Facebook Sponsored Posts?” is a standard question — interviewers score whether you pick a few metrics with a rigorous process, not a long list.
