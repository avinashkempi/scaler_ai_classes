The Analyst Files · Case #12
THE 6 PM RED NUMBER
E-commerce

Setup

You're a Product Analyst at Amazon India. It's Day 3 of the Great Indian Festival. At 6 PM the day-to-date dashboard lands in your inbox, and one number is red. Your manager asks: “Traffic is up 35%, so why aren't sales? Find out what's going on.”

Evidence

  • App and website sessions are up 35% (1.00 crore → 1.35 crore) versus Day 1.
  • Conversion rate (orders ÷ sessions) has dropped from 3.1% to 2.4%.
  • Orders and GMV are up only about 5% — and Day 3 is only part of a day (12 AM–6 PM).

The Mystery

Your manager is waiting. You can ask only one question before you start digging. What do you ask?

Make Your Move

Choose your first move before continuing. There’s no need to know the answer yet — tap an option to see why it’s worth considering.

Why this move

One framework carries you through the whole case: CRIED — Clarify → Rule Out → Internal & External Data — then Conclude by stating the root cause, sizing it, and recommending a fix. TROPiCS drives the Internal Data step.

Your mission: come to class able to explain how a drop that happens all at once differs from a drop that builds slowly.

Section 2

Real-World Use Cases

Festival-sale war roomsDuring Big Billion Days or Great Indian Festival, analysts run exactly this loop every hour — every lost hour of a 10-day sale is lost revenue.
Payment-failure monitoringFintech and e-commerce teams track payment success by method against same-hour baselines, so one failing method shows up in minutes, not at the end of the day.
Mix-shift reportingA big acquisition campaign can pull down overall conversion even when no user converts worse. Reporting new and existing users separately avoids false alarms.
Product analyst interviews“Metric X dropped — investigate” is the most common product-analytics interview question. Interviewers score structure, sizing, and how you communicate the answer.

Section 3

Prerequisites to Refresh

This capstone pulls together almost every lecture in the module. Make sure these are fresh.

CRIED & TROPiCS (RCA I & II)
The spine of today's session. Every checkpoint maps to a CRIED step; TROPiCS decides which slice to look at next.
Direct cause vs. root cause
From the RCA lectures: the direct cause is what you see failing. Keep asking “why?” until you reach what stops it from happening again.
Funnels & conversion
Step-to-step conversion through a purchase funnel, and how a rate is a ratio of two counts (orders ÷ sessions).
Guesstimates
Multiplying a few known quantities to size an impact — lost orders × order value — and stating the time window and whether it's an upper bound.
Metric hierarchy (Product Metrics I)
North Star vs. Primary vs. Supporting metrics. Today you'll pick which supporting metric should have been alerting.
