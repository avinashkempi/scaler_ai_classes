Agentic AI in-Depth

A guided path through the Scaler library for people building AI agents: the lessons that matter most, in order, at the level you need.

What this page is

A study path for agentic AI

The Scaler library has 248 lessons. To build and ship AI agents you don't need all of them, so this page hand-picks the 55 that matter most and puts them in order, at three levels. Every one of them is also in AI/ML Learnings.

Who it is for

Anyone doing the Scaler AWS AI Bootcamp or the AWS AI Capstone who wants to fill a gap or go further than a lesson did: from your first Python script to the maths under embeddings.

How to use it

  1. Pick a level below. Start with Basics if anything in it is new to you.
  2. Work through its lessons in number order; the number is a suggested order.
  3. Each lesson card shows its level and topic and opens the Scaler lesson.
  4. Doing the bootcamp? Open AWS Bootcamp add-ons for the lessons that go with each bootcamp lesson.
