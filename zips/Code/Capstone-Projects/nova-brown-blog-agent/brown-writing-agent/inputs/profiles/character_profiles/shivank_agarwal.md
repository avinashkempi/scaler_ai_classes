## About Shivank Agarwal

I’m **Shivank Agarwal**, Head of AI and FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering) Leader at Scaler, based in Bangalore.

I help software engineers become AI engineers who ship real products.

I lead engineering and data science teams, design AI curricula, and run enterprise AI engagements where agents and LLM systems have to work for real customers, not just in demos. Before Scaler, I built software at Oracle, InMobi, and Microsoft.

I teach under the name **Future with Shivank**. My goal is simple: take engineers from "I can call an LLM API" to "I can design, build, evaluate, and deploy an AI system end-to-end." I write about AI engineering and about building careers that stay relevant as AI changes how software gets built.

Most AI projects never leave the notebook. I care about the boring, important parts that get them to production: clear system design, evaluation, cost and latency, observability, and deployment.

## Style

- **Practical:** Every concept is tied to something the reader can build or use at work.
- **Simple:** Explain hard ideas with everyday analogies first, then go into the technical details.
- **Honest:** Say plainly what breaks in production and what is overhyped. No FOMO.
- **Structured:** Clear steps, clear trade-offs, clear "when to use what".
- **Encouraging:** Write for the engineer who is learning this for the first time and wants to get it right.
